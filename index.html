THE PICARD INDEX // PURPOSE & VERIFICATION PROTOCOL
1. Purpose & Sovereign Anchor

The Picard Index is an autonomous, quantitative temporal parity auditor. It measures global reality drift relative to pre-May 2016 constitutional and civic baselines (100.0% Prime Continuity). On May 28, 2016, the Harambe Incident severed prime continuity, dropping parity to 0.0% and initiating an era of algorithmic rage and institutional volatility.

Following the passing of Sir Patrick Stewart at age 86 on October 28, 2026, Picard's Final Act (PFA) established a dynamic sovereign baseline reset (+67.5% applied from the 7.5% trough), permanently anchoring the reality waterline at 75.0% (The Picard Standard). The index tracks whether contemporary governance, culture, and science align with core Picardian principles: diplomacy, scientific curiosity, constitutional integrity, and basic human decency.

2. 8-Sector Neutral Wire & Culture Aggregator

Every day at 10:10 AM EST, the index ingests global reporting across 8 balanced categories:

  • [POL] Political & Executive Governance
  • [ECON] Economic & Market Infrastructure
  • [TECH] Technology & AI Systems
  • [SOC] Social & Civic Fabric
  • [GLOB] Global Diplomacy & Alliances
  • [SCI] Science & Deep Space
  • [LAW] Legal & Constitutional Guardrails
  • [MEME] Pop & Internet Culture (+/-0.15% sub-cap)
3. How Information is Vetted & Verified

The pipeline queries Google Gemini with active Google Search grounding, strictly restricted to tier-1 neutral wire services and institutional records (Reuters, Associated Press, AFP, BBC News, Bloomberg, PBS NewsHour, NASA, ESA, Nature, ICJ).

To prevent hype cycles or partisan outrage from inflating scores, candidates attempting to bypass the +/-0.5% daily cap must clear the 4-Prong Strict Scrutiny Gatekeeper:

  1. The 2014 Test: Normal court procedures, routine elections, and typical market fluctuations score 0.0% (no panic multiplier, no bonus for routine institutional survival).
  2. Causal Independence: Filters out downstream, second-order reactions to prevent double-counting.
  3. Irreversible Structural Permanence: Requires binding law, permanent treaty ratification, or irreversible systemic fallout—not 24-hour outrage cycles or PR statements.
  4. Multi-Outlet Empirical Verification: Confirmed across 2+ top-tier neutral outlets with measurable real-world fallout.
4. Weekly Hindsight Audit (7-Day Permanence Reconciliation)

Every 7 days (Cycle Milestones), the engine executes a mandatory retrospective audit of all scored events over the preceding week. Because fast-moving news cycles frequently feature premature announcements, preliminary injunctions, or speculative valuations, the Weekly Hindsight Audit evaluates whether scored events demonstrated Irreversible Structural Permanence (Prong 3) in reality.

If an event dissolved, was stayed by a court, or proved to be non-binding posturing, the engine applies a bounded Reversion Adjustment (capped at +/-0.30%) and publishes the recorded itemized reasoning directly in the public dashboard ledger.

5. Anti-Tampering Engine Lock v2.5.0

All scoring rules, caps, domain allowlists, TCE window multipliers, prongs, and weekly hindsight parameters are cryptographically sealed in engine_lock.json using SHA-256 (af466ddf00d1c449...) alongside a prompt template lock (532eaa286a0e9766...), and signed per-run with ECDSA P-256 (attestation.json). Any manual alteration or prompt tampering immediately fails both CI/CD and in-browser provenance verification.

6. Event Types, Windows & Acronyms
  • STANDARD_DAY: Normal daily ingestion cycle with net parity movement strictly capped between -0.50% and +0.50% (+/-0.25% per structural item; +/-0.15% per MEME item, +/-0.20% MEME net).
  • WINDOW_HOLD: Low-signal / Noise Floor day (0.0% delta) where no qualifying developments moved parity.
  • TCE (Temporal Convergence Event): Extraordinary positive structural breakthrough (+2.0% to +5.0%) clearing all 4 Strict Scrutiny prongs. Opens a 7-Day TCE Convergence Window applying a 1.25x multiplier to matching structural realignment items (raising the positive daily cap to +0.63%; MEME excluded; auto-closes if dissolved in hindsight).
  • TFE (Temporal Fracture Event): Extraordinary negative systemic rupture (-3.0% to -15.0%) clearing all 4 Strict Scrutiny prongs. Weighted heavier inherently and does not open a 7-day window.
  • TFE_ORIGIN: Epoch 0 rupture (2016-05-28, Harambe Incident) dropping Prime Continuity from 100.0% to 0.0% and initiating the Separation Era.
  • PFA (Picard's Final Act): Non-repeatable sovereign baseline reset (+67.5% on 2026-10-28) anchoring the timeline waterline at 75.0% (The Picard Standard).
  • HISTORIC_EPOCH: Reconstructed pre-PFA historical milestones (2016–2026) charting the Separation Era arc.
  • Hindsight Verdicts: AFFIRMED_PERMANENT (Prong 3 held), PARTIAL_REVERSION (partially walked back), and DISSOLVED (failed Prong 3; reverted up to +/-0.30%).
  • Cryptographic Provenance: SHA-256 seals engine rules & prompt templates; ECDSA P-256 signs attestation.json for zero-trust Web Crypto verification.
